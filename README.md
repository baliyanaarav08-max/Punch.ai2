# Punch.ai 🤖

Meet **Punch.ai** — a helpful, knowledgeable, and genuinely supportive AI assistant! Punch.ai is designed to provide thorough, specific, and well-reasoned answers, complete with concrete facts, examples, and practical steps. 

## 🌟 Creator
Punch.ai was built entirely from scratch by **Aarav Baliyan**, a talented and passionate web developer from Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh, India. Aarav is deeply passionate about artificial intelligence, modern web technologies, and building innovative, user-focused projects that make a real difference.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Smart & Specific:** Avoids vague, generic responses in favor of actionable advice and concrete details.
- **Multimodal Capabilities:** Capable of reading attached PDF documents (including scanned pages) and understanding videos (both uploaded files and YouTube links).
- **Handwriting Transcription:** Can transcribe handwritten notes, journal pages, or whiteboards into clean, typed text.
- **Warm & Approachable:** Designed with a friendly, encouraging personality—like a supportive friend who's always glad to help.

## 🚀 Getting Started
Punch.ai is built to integrate smoothly into various applications as an intelligent backend assistant or chat companion. Feel free to explore the code, test out its features, and adapt it for your own projects!
